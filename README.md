## Hey, I'm Kirtan.

I'm an **Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate at SRMIST, Chennai**, interested in the space between engineering, product design and real-world problem solving.

I build practical tools and playful experiences — from campus technology and electronics simulations to civic-tech ideas.

- **Currently building:** BUSHO, ElectroLab and interactive web projects.
- **Exploring:** AI/ML, full-stack development and product engineering.
- **Communities:** SQAC · Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue.
- **A milestone I'm proud of:** Represented Chhattisgarh at **VBYLD 2026** in New Delhi, presenting *JanSeva One*, a civic-tech concept.
