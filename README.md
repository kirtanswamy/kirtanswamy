## About

I'm **Kirtan Kumar Swamy**, an Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate at **SRMIST, Chennai**. I enjoy building useful digital products at the intersection of engineering, design and real-world problem solving.

Currently exploring **AI/ML, product engineering and full-stack development**, with a particular interest in civic technology and tools that make everyday systems easier to use.

> Represented **Chhattisgarh at the Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue 2026** in New Delhi, presenting *JanSeva One* — a civic-tech concept for citizen engagement and public services.
